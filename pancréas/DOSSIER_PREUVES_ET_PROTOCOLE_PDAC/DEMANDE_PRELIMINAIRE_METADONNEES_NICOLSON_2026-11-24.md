## Vérifications bibliographiques

Nicolson NG et al., « Patient-derived Organoid Pharmacotyping as a Predictive Tool for Therapeutic Selection in Pancreatic Ductal Adenocarcinoma », *Annals of Surgery* 283(6), e8–e18 (numéro imprimé 2026; publication électronique le 4 septembre 2024), DOI `10.1097/SLA.0000000000006517`, PMID `39229726`. PubMed rapporte 95 PDO (21 issus de biopsies d'un essai clinique et 74 de spécimens chirurgicaux).

Ces renseignements décrivent l'étude publiée; ils ne démontrent pas que des données brutes par dose, des expériences indépendantes appariées ou des réplications biologiques conformes au protocole V1.0 existent ou peuvent être partagées.

## Brouillon de courriel en anglais

**Subject:** Metadata-only inquiry: PDAC PDO drug-response data and replication structure

Dear Dr. Nicolson and colleagues,

I am assessing whether the PDAC patient-derived organoid pharmacotyping study could support a future, independently governed methodological evaluation of dose-response curve estimation. I am referring to your *Annals of Surgery* article, “Patient-derived Organoid Pharmacotyping as a Predictive Tool for Therapeutic Selection in Pancreatic Ductal Adenocarcinoma” (DOI: 10.1097/SLA.0000000000006517; PMID: 39229726).

At this preliminary stage, I am **not requesting response values, patient-level information, or access to any test outcomes**. A brief metadata-only response would be sufficient to determine whether the dataset could be assessed without compromising an independent test.

Could you please indicate, at a high level and without sharing response values:

1. Whether individual measurements by drug concentration are retained, or whether only summary pharmacotypes/AUC/IC50 values are available.
2. The approximate number of PDO models/donors and drug/model combinations with raw dose-level measurements, and the number of distinct concentrations per curve.
3. Whether measurements are available separately for biological experiments/cultures and technical wells, with a data dictionary describing the replicate hierarchy.
4. Whether, for any same PDO–compound pair, a short curve and a full reference curve were acquired in separate, independently cultured experiments/plates; if so, whether at least three independent biological experiments are available for each arm.
5. Whether a coded, non-identifying manifest and data dictionary could be shared before any response values, including dose units, assay endpoint/timepoint, vehicle-control normalization, plate/batch identifiers, replicate definitions, exclusions, and missingness.
6. Whether data access would require institutional approvals, an IRB determination, a data-use agreement, or other restrictions, and whether a suitably independent custodian could retain the response values until a protocol and analysis code are locked.

The proposed evaluation criteria are still being finalized with independent statistical input. No confirmatory analysis has been run on your data, and no clinical conclusions are being sought. If this dataset is not available in a suitable form, a short indication of that would also be very helpful.

Thank you for your time,

[Name]

[Institution / project affiliation]
