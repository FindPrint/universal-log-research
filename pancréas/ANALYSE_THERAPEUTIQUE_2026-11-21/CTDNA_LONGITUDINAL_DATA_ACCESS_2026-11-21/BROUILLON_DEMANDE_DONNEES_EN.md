**Subject:** Request for de-identified longitudinal ctDNA and response data from the metastatic pancreatic cancer cohort

Dear Corresponding Author,

I am conducting an exploratory, reproducibility-focused analysis of published longitudinal data in metastatic pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma. Your study reports serial plasma collection before treatment and every two weeks until restaging, with ctDNA measurements and radiological response outcomes. The article states that the raw data supporting its conclusions can be made available by the authors.

Would you be willing to share a de-identified patient-by-sample table, data dictionary, and any applicable data-use conditions? The analysis would benefit from the following fields where available:

- stable pseudonymous patient key and sample key;
- time relative to treatment start (for example, week number), without direct identifiers;
- ctDNA mutant allele frequency or quantitative result, assay/variant tested, detection status, limit of detection, and missingness reason;
- cfDNA quantity and units;
- treatment regimen, line, and sample timing relative to cycles;
- radiological response category at restaging and its assessment time;
- CA19-9, tumor volume, or SLD measurements if already available;
- PFS/OS durations with event/censoring indicators, if permitted;
- variable definitions, units, cohort inclusion flow, and any corrections needed to reproduce the published analysis.

We would follow your preferred controlled-transfer method, comply with the applicable data-use terms, preserve confidentiality, and report the study's original definitions and limitations. We would not use the analysis to guide individual treatment decisions. Please let me know whether institutional approval, a data-use agreement, or a more specific proposal is required.
