# Unsent data request draft — Edland et al. 2023

**Status: draft only; not sent.**

**Subject:** Request for de-identified longitudinal ctDNA data and data dictionary (Edland et al., 2023)

Dear Professor Nordgård and colleagues,

I am conducting a reproducibility-focused analysis of published longitudinal data in advanced pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma. Your 2023 study reports serial ctDNA measurements in 81 patients and 425 plasma samples. I could not locate a downloadable patient-by-sample table among the article files I reviewed.

Would you be able to share, or point me to, a de-identified table containing one row per patient and sampling visit, with a stable pseudonymous patient key, time from baseline, ctDNA measurement and units, ctDNA detection status, CA19-9 where available, and the corresponding clinical progression/response fields? A data dictionary and details on missingness, assay transformations, permitted use, and citation requirements would be very helpful. I do not need direct identifiers or dates of birth.

The intended first step is a transparent method-compatibility audit. Repeated samples would remain grouped by patient; they would not be concatenated and treated as independent patients or as one uninterrupted biological trajectory. I would share the proposed protocol and follow any institutional, ethics, or data-use requirements you specify before analysis or redistribution.

If individual data cannot be shared, a synthetic example with the same schema, or a recommended controlled-access route, would still help us assess reproducibility without disclosing participant-level information.

Thank you for considering this request.
